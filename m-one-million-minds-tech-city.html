M One at Million Minds Tech City Ahmedabad

M One is the first major office-tower proposition highlighted within Million Minds Tech City. Public developer material positions it for IT, ITeS, GCC, enterprise, R&D and technology-led occupiers seeking large, flexible Grade A workplace formats in Ahmedabad.

Large-floor workplace planning

A published carpet floor plate of approximately 42,000 sq. ft. can support teams that value contiguous seating, efficient circulation and fewer split-floor dependencies. Actual leasable, chargeable and usable areas must be confirmed for the specific unit under consideration.

Systems and sustainability

Developer material refers to full power backup, high-efficiency cooling, EV charging and smart building systems. It also describes the development as targeting or pre-certified for IGBC Platinum in different materials; prospects should request the current certificate and exact scope before using the rating in a corporate decision.

What to verify before leasing

Request the current test-fit, structural grid, lift schedule, electrical load, redundancy design, HVAC basis, parking allocation, handover condition and fit-out rules. These operational details matter more than a headline specification viewed in isolation.
