Corporate Office Space in Ahmedabad

A corporate office must satisfy business strategy, workforce needs, infrastructure and financial governance at the same time. This guide helps occupiers turn headcount into a clear requirement, compare Ahmedabad locations fairly and run premises-level technical and commercial diligence.

By PIKORUA Realty Research Desk · Updated 12 September 2026

Create a decision-ready office brief

Document current and future headcount, attendance assumptions, business units, shift patterns, security zones, meeting demand, amenities, accessibility and target possession. Include a low, base and growth scenario.

Translate the programme into carpet and usable space through a test-fit. Avoid applying a universal square-foot-per-seat ratio without support-space and circulation assumptions.

Choose location from business needs

Map employee origins and hiring zones, client and leadership travel, airport or intercity needs, operating hours and resilience dependencies. Score SG Highway, other Ahmedabad corridors and the Gandhinagar or GIFT City context only where relevant.

Million Minds Tech City can be assessed as an off-SG-Highway enterprise option behind Nirma University, subject to live inventory and premises-specific evidence.

Validate the building

Review structure, floor geometry, lifts, power, backup, cooling, fresh air, carrier diversity, fire safety, accessibility, parking, loading, visitor management and facility operations.

Obtain current certificates, approved plans and system schedules. Marketing classifications such as Grade A should be translated into measurable requirements.

Control delivery risk

Clarify base-build handover, landlord and tenant works, approvals, design freeze, procurement, testing, commissioning and occupancy dependencies. Connect rent commencement to realistic delivery milestones.

Plan business continuity, move sequencing and expansion before signing, especially for large or phased teams.

Normalise the commercial offer

Compare area definitions, rent, escalation, deposit, lock-in, maintenance, parking, taxes, fit-out period and reinstatement consistently. Add fit-out, technology, furniture, utilities, transport and internal management costs.

Document critical representations and conditions in the final legal package. A presentation or web page is not a substitute for the agreement.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

How much office space does a corporate team need?

It depends on attendance, workplace standards, support functions and growth; commission a test-fit using real programme data.

What is Grade A corporate office space?

It is a market label. Define and verify the location, systems, safety, efficiency, accessibility and management standards the business requires.

Can Million Minds Tech City be shortlisted?

Yes, for relevant enterprise requirements, subject to current availability, technical diligence and commercial fit.

Which stakeholders should approve a site?

Typically business, HR, real estate, workplace, IT, security, facilities, finance, legal and tax or compliance where relevant.

What is total occupancy cost?

The combined lifecycle cost of premises, fit-out, operations, technology, transport, utilities, taxes and exit obligations.
