Sources & Verification Methodology

We use a source hierarchy and dated status labels so readers can distinguish official specifications, corporate disclosures, media reporting and independent analysis.

By PIKORUA Realty Research Desk · Updated 1 September 2026

Source hierarchy

Priority order: official project or developer documentation; stock-exchange and regulatory filings; government sources; company announcements; reputable national business media; high-quality secondary research.

Verification labels

“Published” describes a source’s statement. “Reported” does not confirm operational status. “Planned” is not treated as delivered. Live price and availability claims require current written confirmation.

Review process

Each important page carries an updated date and source list. Conflicts are resolved in favour of the more authoritative and recent source, with cautious wording where uncertainty remains.
