Editorial Policy

Our editorial goal is to publish useful, clear and verifiable information for commercial-property decision-makers while separating facts, public reporting, analysis and leasing communication.

Standards

We prefer primary developer, company, regulatory and government documents, followed by reputable business media. We do not invent prices, occupiers, certifications, distances or availability.

Corrections and updates

Material changes are reviewed against sources and reflected in the visible updated date. Promotional copy does not override evidence.

AI assistance

Technology may assist drafting and structure, but factual claims require human review against cited material before publication.
