Office Space Near the GIFT City Corridor

Million Minds Tech City is in Ahmedabad off SG Highway; it is not inside GIFT City. It may be considered by teams evaluating the wider Ahmedabad–Gandhinagar business corridor, but the two locations have different regulatory, ecosystem and commute contexts.

Do not treat the locations as interchangeable

GIFT City has a distinct financial and regulatory ecosystem. An Ahmedabad office can serve a different talent, client and operational footprint. Compare entity requirements, employee origins and sector-specific benefits before deciding.

Questions for regional teams

Assess leadership travel, airport access, employee commute, partner proximity, resilience, expansion capacity and whether the business needs to be physically located within GIFT City for regulatory or ecosystem reasons.

Why SG Highway may enter the shortlist

SG Highway offers access to established Ahmedabad catchments and routes toward Gandhinagar. Million Minds Tech City is positioned as a technology-led integrated development within this wider corridor.
