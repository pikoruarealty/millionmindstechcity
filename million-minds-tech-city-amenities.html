Amenities described for M One

The developer lists a grand double-height reception, visitor lounge, restaurant, food court, cafeteria, gymnasium, crèche, entertainment zone and landscaped podium-level areas. Confirm opening status, access rules, capacity and any user charges during due diligence.

Business continuity

Published material refers to 100% power backup, high-efficiency cooling, smart systems and facility management. Occupiers should request service-level details and technical schedules that apply to their premises.

Future mixed-use context

The wider plan includes offices, residential, co-living, hospitality and retail. Phasing matters: a master-plan feature should not be treated as currently delivered unless confirmed in writing.
