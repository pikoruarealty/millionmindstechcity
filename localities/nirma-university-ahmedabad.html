Why this landmark matters

The developer's current project information identifies the development as behind Nirma University, off SG Highway. That makes the university a useful search and navigation landmark while the complete project address remains the appropriate reference for formal purposes.

Property portals may use the landmark broadly. Always check the exact pin, site boundary, entrance and legal premises address before comparing alternatives.

From landmark search to office brief

Define headcount, growth, target carpet or chargeable area, preferred possession condition, shift pattern, power density, cooling, security, parking and expansion needs before requesting options. A precise brief prevents a landmark search from producing an unfocused list.

For large teams, include test-fit assumptions and departmental adjacencies. A floor's marketed area tells little about practical capacity until core, columns, circulation, collaboration and support space are modelled.

Access and employee experience

Run commute analysis using the homes and hiring zones relevant to the team. Test peak and off-peak movement, staff transport, last-mile safety, food and daily services, accessibility and late-shift arrangements.

Do not publish or rely on a single fixed travel time. Route conditions and access gates can change, while different employee groups may experience the same location very differently.

Enterprise building diligence

Ask for approved plans, handover status, power topology, generator interface, HVAC basis, fresh-air rates, carrier options, lifts, fire and life-safety documents, parking allocation and fit-out rules.

Sustainability or Grade A claims should be connected to current certificates and defined scope. Determine whether a claim applies to a tower, phase, shell design or operating building.

Compare proposals on equal terms

Standardise the area basis, rent commencement, fit-out period, escalation, deposit, lock-in, maintenance, parking, utilities and taxes. Include workplace construction, technology, transport and change costs in the business case.

Confirm every time-sensitive item—inventory, rate, handover and amenity availability—in a current written proposal. Online content is useful for research but cannot replace transaction documents.
