Zundal Ahmedabad Office Location Guide

Zundal can be relevant when organisations map residential catchments and office access in north Ahmedabad. This page does not represent Million Minds Tech City as being in Zundal; it explains how to test the locality as one part of a broader commute and workplace decision anchored to the project's verified SG Highway address.

Use Zundal as a catchment, not an address shortcut

Locality keywords often cover a wider geography than formal project addresses. Million Minds Tech City's public location remains behind Nirma University, off SG Highway; Zundal should be used only where it genuinely helps assess access, workforce or housing context.

For navigation, distribute a verified project pin and current gate instructions. For contracts and registrations, use the exact address in primary documentation.

Analyse actual employee origins

Group employees by residential cluster and shift, then sample likely routes at real travel times. Review company transport feasibility, pickup consolidation, driver access, last-mile lighting and the experience for two-wheelers and pedestrians.

Future hiring can matter as much as current staff. HR should assess which talent pools are relevant by role rather than assuming proximity to one residential area solves recruitment.

Separate location from building quality

A favourable route does not compensate for weak power, cooling, safety, telecom or floor efficiency. Run a technical review of the specific building and unit, including service capacity at planned occupancy.

Confirm whether amenities and mixed-use components are delivered, restricted to tenants, charged separately or planned for a later phase.

Plan for changing travel patterns

Roadworks, new development and occupancy growth can alter traffic. Use scenarios instead of a permanent journey promise, and review routing as headcount or shift patterns change.

If company buses are expected, validate turning, staging, pickup and after-hours access with the facility team before signing.

Document the final decision

Use a weighted scorecard covering talent reach, commute, resilience, total occupancy cost, handover, brand, client access and expansion. Note the evidence source and date for every significant assumption.

Before commitment, reconcile the scorecard with legal, commercial and technical documents for the exact premises.

Frequently asked questions

Is Million Minds Tech City located in Zundal?

The public project location is behind Nirma University, off SG Highway. Zundal is discussed as a nearby-area and commute-research context.

Why evaluate Zundal for an office decision?

It may be relevant to employee residential catchments and transport planning, depending on the workforce.

Should we use online travel times?

Use them only as a starting point and repeat live checks for actual shift windows.

What should HR contribute?

Role-based hiring zones, employee-origin data, shift patterns, attrition risk and transport policy.

What must the leasing team confirm?

Exact unit, area basis, handover, rate, parking, access and technical provisions in writing.
