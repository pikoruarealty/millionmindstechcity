Office Space on S.G. Highway, Ahmedabad

SG Highway is one of Ahmedabad’s best-known commercial corridors, linking established western business districts with northern growth areas and Gandhinagar. Million Minds Tech City adds a large technology-focused office proposition off this corridor.

What makes the corridor relevant

The corridor connects corporate, residential, education, healthcare and hospitality catchments. Relevance depends on the workforce and client map of each organisation, so employee commute analysis should precede a final shortlist.

Compare buildings consistently

Use the same area definition, lease term, escalation, maintenance, parking and fit-out assumptions. Compare floor efficiency, power, cooling, lifts, telecom, safety and handover conditions alongside the headline rent.

Plan for growth

Model at least two headcount scenarios and ask whether expansion can occur contiguously or in later phases. A lower initial cost can become expensive if future teams are fragmented.
