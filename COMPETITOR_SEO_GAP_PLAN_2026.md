# Million Minds Tech City Competitive SEO Gap Plan

Audit date: 12 September 2026  
Our site: https://www.millionmindstechcity.in/  
Compared site: https://www.millionmindstechcity.com/  
Positioning: independent information and leasing-assistance website; not the official developer website.

## Executive conclusion

The `.com` site has the strongest natural advantage: it is the official project domain and is reinforced by Ganesh Housing, its brochure and policy documents, events, media coverage and official social profiles. The `.in` site should not copy or impersonate it. Our best route to visibility is to become the most useful independent answer for brand research, location, M One specifications, reported occupiers, due diligence and leasing intent.

The local `.in` homepage is now technically stronger than the sampled `.com` homepage: concise metadata, one entity-first H1, five JSON-LD blocks, explicit social metadata, a 1200×630 branded preview, fewer images and visible source citations. Authority, originality and real-world evidence—not adding more generic pages—remain the main ranking constraint.

No ethical SEO implementation can guarantee position one. Ranking depends on Google, query/location/device, official-domain authority, links, content satisfaction and competitor changes. Success must be measured in Search Console.

## Competitor strengths we must answer

1. **Official entity authority:** exact-match official domain, developer relationship and official contact identity.
2. **First-party proof:** brochure, IT/GCC/SEZ/IGBC documents and technical Phase 1 material.
3. **Freshness and PR:** launch coverage, events, roadshows and media-center pages.
4. **Commercial proof:** ready-to-lease messaging, enterprise positioning and direct enquiry fields.
5. **External reinforcement:** Ganesh Housing, press coverage and official social profiles support the same entity.

## Competitor gaps we can exploit

1. The sampled homepage title and description are substantially longer than normal search-snippet ranges.
2. The homepage H1 is informational and does not include Ahmedabad.
3. No JSON-LD or Open Graph image was detected in the sampled source.
4. The homepage ships a large HTML document and many images.
5. The sitemap gives many HTML pages priority `1.0` and includes PDFs; priority is not a substitute for information architecture.
6. An official site naturally promotes the project. An independent site can provide clearer comparison, verification status, leasing due diligence and source-labelled caveats.

## Ranking plan

### P0 — deploy and measure (days 1–3)

1. Deploy this repository and confirm the live HTML title, description, H1, canonical, social image, schema and favicon.
2. Submit `https://www.millionmindstechcity.in/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
3. Inspect `/`, `/m-one-million-minds-tech-city`, `/million-minds-tech-city-office-space`, `/million-minds-tech-city-address-location`, `/million-minds-tech-city-companies` and `/million-minds-tech-city-specifications` in URL Inspection.
4. Connect GA4 and record verified-lead, form-submit, click-to-call and email-click conversions.
5. Record a baseline export for queries, pages, countries, devices, indexed pages and Core Web Vitals.

### P1 — build evidence competitors cannot copy (weeks 1–3)

1. Replace generic/representational building visuals with original, rights-cleared project photography and descriptive captions.
2. Publish a dated M One technical due-diligence table: carpet/chargeable area, floor height, HVAC, power, lifts, parking, floor loading, certifications, fit-out and possession status. Every commercial claim needs a source and verification date.
3. Maintain the occupier tracker with separate labels: announced, leased, fit-out, operational and unverified.
4. Add an office requirement calculator or downloadable occupier checklist that genuinely helps enterprise teams.
5. Publish named author/reviewer profiles with commercial-real-estate experience and correction contact details.
6. Strengthen the six P0 pages before publishing new pages. Merge or expand weak pages if Search Console shows overlap.

### P1 — earn authority (weeks 2–8)

1. Obtain legitimate citations from the operator's company site, broker profiles, business directories and relevant Ahmedabad/Gujarat property ecosystems.
2. Pitch source-led data stories—not keyword guest posts—around GCC office demand, floor-plate planning, SG Highway and verified occupier milestones.
3. Seek accurate link attribution when third parties use original data, photography or market tables.
4. Keep name, address, phone and independent-site wording consistent wherever the operator is listed.

### P2 — improve performance and conversion (weeks 2–4)

1. Convert large gallery JPGs to responsive AVIF/WebP and keep the hero image within the LCP budget.
2. Test the six-second lead popup against a non-blocking variant. Measure qualified leads and exits, not submissions alone.
3. Give priority landing pages distinct 1200×630 social cards.
4. Validate mobile Core Web Vitals with Search Console field data after deployment.

## 90-day success criteria

- Priority pages indexed and canonicalized correctly.
- Growth in non-branded impressions and clicks for office, M One, location and specification queries.
- Improved average position for query clusters, assessed over 28-day comparisons rather than daily checks.
- More verified/qualified leads per organic landing session.
- More relevant referring domains and branded mentions.
- No manual actions, unsupported schema, misleading official claims or unverified occupier statements.
