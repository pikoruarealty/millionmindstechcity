Million Minds Tech City & M One Specifications

Public project information highlights large floor plates, high floor-to-floor dimensions, backup power, efficient cooling and smart-building features. This guide records the published headline specifications while identifying the documents an occupier should request before commitment.

Area and test-fit

Request dimensioned plans showing carpet, usable and chargeable area; column grid; core; toilets; refuge and service zones. Test-fits should reflect real circulation, collaboration, support and growth space rather than desk count alone.

MEP and resilience

Confirm sanctioned and backup power, power density, UPS and generator interface, HVAC design, fresh-air provision, telecom pathways, water systems and maintenance responsibilities. Ask which systems are base-building provisions and which are tenant scope.

Vertical transport, parking and access

Review passenger and service-lift capacity, destination control if applicable, peak-time assumptions, parking allocation, visitor management, loading and after-hours access. Written schedules should prevail over marketing summaries.
