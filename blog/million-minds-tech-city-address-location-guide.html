Verified public location

The current developer page lists: Behind Nirma University, Off SG Highway, Ahmedabad, Gujarat – 382470. Because entrance arrangements can change, request the current visitor pin when booking.

Regional context

The site is near Vaishnodevi Circle within Ahmedabad’s northern SG Highway corridor. It is connected to the wider Ahmedabad–Gandhinagar region but is not located inside GIFT City.

Before travelling

Confirm the appointment time, entrance, contact person and parking instructions. Check live traffic for the day rather than relying on permanent drive-time claims.

Location questions for employees and visitors

Map employee origins by real shift patterns, not only office hours. Review public and company transport, last-mile safety, visitor wayfinding, delivery access and emergency routes. For senior leadership and customer travel, test the airport and intercity journey with live conditions.

Landmarks versus legal address

A familiar landmark such as Nirma University or Vaishnodevi Circle is useful for orientation, but contracts and compliance records need the precise legal premises description. Ask for the correct tower, floor, survey or plot references and postal details in formal documents.
