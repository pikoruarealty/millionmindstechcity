Commercial Office Lease Checklist for Ahmedabad

A leasing decision combines legal rights, technical performance, delivery and lifecycle cost. This checklist organises the questions an occupier should assign across real estate, legal, finance, tax, workplace, IT, security and facilities before signing an Ahmedabad office lease.

By PIKORUA Realty Research Desk · Updated 12 September 2026
WorkstreamsLegal, commercial, technical, operational and delivery
EvidenceCurrent documents for the exact premises
AdviceUse qualified legal, tax and technical professionals

Property and legal review

Confirm the contracting entity, authority to lease, title and encumbrance review, approved use, premises description, access rights, common areas and any SEZ or regulatory status.

Map every referenced plan, approval and schedule to the exact tower, floor and unit. This guide is not legal advice.

Area and commercial terms

Define carpet, usable and chargeable area and the measurement document that controls. Review rent, escalation, deposit, lock-in, notice, maintenance, parking, utilities, taxes and payment triggers.

Identify rent-free or fit-out periods, conditions precedent, delay protection and which amounts remain payable before operations begin.

Technical capability

Validate power, backup, cooling, fresh air, connectivity, lifts, fire safety, accessibility, water, parking, loading and security against the planned density and shifts.

Require technical schedules and test or commissioning evidence where performance is critical.

Fit-out and handover

Agree possession condition, landlord works, design standards, approval workflow, contractor access, working hours, deposits, insurance, testing, snagging and acceptance.

Create a responsibility matrix and programme that connects handover, fit-out, IT, statutory approvals and business move dates.

Operations, expansion and exit

Review service levels, after-hours operation, business continuity, signage, access, audit rights, insurance, damage, assignment, subletting, expansion, contraction, reinstatement and handback.

Record all negotiated commitments in the executed documents rather than relying on emails or presentations alone.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Can an online checklist replace a lawyer?

No. Use qualified legal, tax and technical advisors for the specific transaction.

Why does area definition matter?

It determines occupancy capacity and the base on which rent and other charges may be calculated.

What is a condition precedent?

A requirement that must be satisfied before a contractual obligation or milestone becomes effective, as defined by the agreement.

Should technical diligence happen before commercial agreement?

It should inform negotiations before binding commitment because system gaps can affect cost, timing and suitability.

What should be written into the lease?

All material rights, obligations, specifications, milestones, remedies, costs and exit arrangements.
