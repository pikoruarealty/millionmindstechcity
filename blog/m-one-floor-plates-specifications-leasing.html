Published headline specifications

Developer material currently references a 42,000+ sq. ft. carpet floor plate, approximately 4.2-metre floor height and a 16-storey tower. These figures are a starting point, not a substitute for unit documents.

From floor plate to test-fit

A workplace test-fit should model seats, collaboration, focus, support, circulation, security zones and growth. Confirm carpet, usable and chargeable definitions before comparing alternatives.

Technical diligence

Review power, cooling, fresh air, telecom pathways, lifts, parking, loading, fire and life safety, fit-out rules and current sustainability certification scope.

Commercial questions behind the area

Ask whether proposals quote carpet, usable, built-up or chargeable area; which common-area loading applies; and how balconies, service zones or terraces are treated. Model rent, maintenance and fit-out cost against the same area basis.

Delivery and expansion planning

Confirm handover condition, fit-out approval timelines, working-hour rules, contractor access, material movement and commissioning responsibilities. For phased teams, document rights over adjacent space and the contingency if future floors are unavailable.
