The project in brief

Million Minds Tech City is presented by Ganesh Housing as a 65-acre integrated technology and mixed-use development off SG Highway, behind Nirma University. The public plan combines Grade A offices with future residential, co-living, hospitality and retail components.

M One and workplace scale

M One is promoted as an early office tower with a 42,000+ sq. ft. carpet floor plate and approximately 4.2-metre floor height. Prospects should confirm unit-specific measurements, current availability and certification documents.

How to research it responsibly

Separate currently delivered facilities from future master-plan components, publicly reported occupiers from operating companies, and promotional claims from legal specifications. Use the linked address, companies, specifications and leasing pages for focused checks.

Key facts that need dated verification

The official project page currently describes 18+ million sq. ft. of integrated development across 65 acres, including more than 10 million sq. ft. of tech park space. Those are master-plan figures, not a statement that every component is delivered today. Record the source date whenever using them in a board paper.

A practical occupier workflow

First confirm location and unit status. Next commission a test-fit and technical review. Then compare a written commercial proposal on a like-for-like basis. Finally, verify approvals, handover dependencies and expansion rights before committing. This sequence keeps branding and imagery from obscuring operational risk.
