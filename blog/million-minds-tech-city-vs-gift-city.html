The essential distinction

Million Minds Tech City is off SG Highway in Ahmedabad. GIFT City is a separate business district with its own ecosystem and, for eligible entities, a distinct regulatory context. “Near GIFT City” should never be read as “inside GIFT City.”

What to compare

Assess regulatory needs, client proximity, employee catchments, leadership travel, sector ecosystem, building options, total occupancy cost and expansion plans. The right answer can differ across business units.

Choose from the operating model

A financial-services entity evaluating GIFT-specific benefits has different requirements from a domestic technology delivery centre. Obtain legal and tax advice where location affects approvals or incentives.

Create a weighted comparison

Score each location on regulatory fit, talent, commute, customers, infrastructure, building choice, cost, resilience and expansion. Agree the weights with finance, HR, tax, technology and business leaders before viewing properties so the decision does not become image-led.

When a two-location model may work

Some organisations separate regulated or client-facing functions from larger technology or operations teams. A split model can improve fit but adds management, security, travel and culture complexity. Test those costs before assuming two sites are automatically more resilient.
