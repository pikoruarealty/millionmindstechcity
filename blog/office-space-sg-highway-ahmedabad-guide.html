Why SG Highway stays on shortlists

The corridor links western Ahmedabad with northern growth areas and Gandhinagar while serving established residential, corporate, education, healthcare and hospitality catchments.

A fair property comparison

Normalise carpet and chargeable area, lease term, escalation, maintenance, parking, taxes and fit-out. Then compare floor efficiency, resilience, access and expansion—not rent alone.

Make the site visit useful

Bring real-estate, workplace, IT, facilities and security stakeholders. Test the last mile and record every technical or commercial statement that needs written confirmation.

Build a total-occupancy-cost model

Include rent, common-area maintenance, parking, electricity and demand charges, taxes, insurance, fit-out amortisation, transport, security, technology links and reinstatement. Use best, expected and downside occupancy scenarios.

Protect the expansion plan

Ask whether growth can be accommodated on the same floor, in the same tower or only in a future phase. Review rights of first offer, delivery dates and subdivision options. Fragmented growth can affect security, culture and operating cost.
