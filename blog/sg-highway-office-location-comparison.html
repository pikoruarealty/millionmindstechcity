How to Compare Office Locations on SG Highway Ahmedabad

SG Highway is a corridor, not one uniform office market. Buildings can differ in last-mile access, workforce reach, systems, handover and cost. A weighted scorecard lets decision-makers compare them consistently and explain why one option suits the operating model.

By PIKORUA Realty Research Desk · Updated 12 September 2026
ScorecardAgree criteria and weights before touring
EvidenceUse workforce data, route tests and property documents
OutcomeCompare value and risk, not rent alone

Set the business context

Document roles, headcount, shifts, customer activity, leadership travel, security, technical density, timeline and growth. These requirements determine the weights.

A GCC, regional headquarters, managed project team and showroom should not use the same generic location scorecard.

Score talent and commute

Use employee-origin and hiring data, timed route samples, public or company transport feasibility, parking and last-mile safety. Consider how access changes across shifts.

Avoid giving every residential catchment equal weight; focus on the roles and teams most important to the operation.

Score building and delivery

Assess floor efficiency, power, cooling, telecom, lifts, safety, accessibility, amenities, facility management, fit-out controls, approvals and handover certainty.

Separate commissioned capacity from future or marketing commitments and note the evidence date.

Score commercial value

Normalise area, rent, maintenance, parking, taxes, escalation, deposit and fit-out periods. Add build cost, technology, transport, utilities, overlap and internal programme costs.

Run downside scenarios for delay, slower headcount ramp and future expansion.

Use the result with judgement

The scorecard structures debate but does not replace site visits, references or professional diligence. Review sensitivities to see whether the winner changes when key weights move.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

What criteria should an SG Highway office scorecard include?

Talent, commute, clients, infrastructure, readiness, safety, employee experience, total cost and expansion.

Who should set the weights?

A cross-functional team representing business, HR, real estate, IT, security, facilities and finance.

How many options should be compared?

Enough credible alternatives to test the market, while removing options that fail mandatory requirements.

Should rent have the highest weight?

Only if the business explicitly decides so; total value and operational risk are usually broader.

How should Million Minds Tech City be scored?

Against the same evidence-based criteria and with current premises-specific information.
