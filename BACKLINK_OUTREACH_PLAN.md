# Backlink and Citation Outreach Plan

## Identity rule

All profiles and citations must use the real operator identity, PIKORUA Realty. Do not create a listing that impersonates Million Minds Tech City or Ganesh Housing. Use a project address only when the operator is genuinely eligible and authorised to represent a business at that address.

## Priority channels

1. Claim or update the real operator's Google Business Profile only if eligible under Google's business-representation rules.
2. Create consistent real-business records in Bing Places and Apple Business.
3. Maintain the operator's LinkedIn company page and publish original Ahmedabad workplace research.
4. Publish useful location, specification and comparison videos on YouTube with links to the relevant canonical page.
5. Use Justdial or Sulekha only for the actual service business and real NAP/service-area details.

## Linkable assets

- Source-backed Million Minds Tech City occupier tracker.
- M One technical specification and verification table.
- Ahmedabad GCC office-location guide.

## Outreach workflow

1. Identify company, workplace, GCC, Ahmedabad business, architecture and sustainability publishers already covering the topic.
2. Find a factual gap, outdated statement or missing source in a relevant article.
3. Send a concise correction or offer a reusable sourced dataset—not a generic backlink request.
4. Point to the single most relevant deep page with a natural anchor suggestion.
5. Record contact, date, response, resulting citation and whether information remains accurate.

## Prohibited tactics

- Bulk backlink packages, PBNs or paid dofollow networks.
- Automated profile, forum or comment spam.
- Fake local listings, reviews or tenant claims.
- Exact-match anchor campaigns.
- Full-article syndication that competes with the canonical source.
