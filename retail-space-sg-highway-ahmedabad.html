Retail Space on SG Highway Ahmedabad

Retail-space selection should connect the target customer and operating model to a specific unit. SG Highway offers varied commercial contexts, so brands need evidence about access, catchment, co-tenancy, visibility, services and use approvals rather than a generic corridor claim.

Match the space to the retail format

Define whether the concept is convenience, food and beverage, service, experience, destination retail or employee-serving amenity. The right floor, frontage, opening hours and parking pattern depend on that role.

A retail unit within an office-led development may draw weekday employees differently from a street-facing destination store. Build demand assumptions from the actual mix and phasing.

Study access and footfall quality

Count relevant customers rather than total movement. Observe pedestrian paths, vehicle entry, drop-off, visibility from both directions, barriers, weather exposure and accessibility.

If the development is phased, separate current occupied catchment from future master-plan capacity. Forecasts should show the dates and dependencies behind each demand scenario.

Technical and statutory suitability

Confirm permitted use, fire and life safety, occupancy permissions, signage, electrical load, water, drainage, exhaust, gas, waste handling, loading and operating-hour restrictions.

Use-specific requirements should be reviewed by qualified designers and advisors before a commercial commitment.

Lease and operating economics

Compare carpet and chargeable area, base rent, revenue share if any, maintenance, marketing levies, utilities, parking, taxes, deposits, escalation and fit-out period.

Model store construction, staffing, logistics, wastage and realistic ramp-up. A location should work under conservative assumptions, not only an optimistic footfall case.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is retail space the same as showroom space?

Not always. Retail formats can include convenience, food, services and daily-needs uses, while showrooms often prioritise display, destination visits and larger frontage.

Is retail available at Million Minds Tech City now?

Confirm current unit-level availability and delivery in writing; public master-plan references alone are insufficient.

What should a food operator check?

Permitted use, exhaust, gas, water, drainage, waste, loading, fire safety, hours and customer access.

How should footfall be measured?

Observe relevant customer movement by time and day, then distinguish current demand from forecast future occupancy.

What costs should the model include?

Rent, common charges, taxes, utilities, parking, fit-out, marketing levies, staffing, logistics and working capital.
