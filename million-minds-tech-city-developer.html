Who Developed Million Minds Tech City?

Ganesh Housing Limited publicly identifies itself as the developer of Million Minds Tech City. Project material also names Tishman Speyer in development-management context and RSP Architects in architectural-design context. Roles should be described exactly as the latest source states.

Developer context

Ganesh Housing presents Million Minds Tech City as a 65-acre integrated development off SG Highway in Ahmedabad, planned around technology offices and supporting mixed-use components.

Why partner roles matter

Developer, development manager, architect, contractor, operator and leasing advisor are different roles. Clear attribution helps occupiers understand accountability and prevents an independent information site from claiming ownership or official status.
