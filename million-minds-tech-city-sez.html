Million Minds Tech City SEZ Explained

Developer and corporate material describes Million Minds Tech City in an IT/ITeS SEZ context. SEZ suitability is company-specific and can affect unit approval, authorised operations, customs, taxation, compliance and exit planning, so professional advice is essential.

What occupiers should verify

Confirm whether the specific premises sit within a notified and operational SEZ area, which authorised operations apply, what approvals are required and whether the company’s proposed activities qualify. Do not assume every building or mixed-use component shares the same status.

SEZ versus non-SEZ

An SEZ can be relevant for eligible export-oriented operations, while non-SEZ space may suit domestic operations or businesses seeking simpler compliance. The right choice depends on business model, tax position, customer contracts, talent, timing and expansion plans.

Document checklist

Request current notification and approval references, unit-setup process, customs and access rules, utility and common-service structure, exit conditions and professional advice tailored to the entity.
