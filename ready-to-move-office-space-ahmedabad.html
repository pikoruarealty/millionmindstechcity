Ready-to-Move Office Space in Ahmedabad

Ready-to-move should mean more than an attractive furnished floor. Occupiers must verify what is installed, tested, approved, included and capable of supporting the planned team. This checklist separates cosmetic readiness from genuine operational readiness.

By PIKORUA Realty Research Desk · Updated 12 September 2026

Critical evidenceInventory, test reports, approvals and handover schedule

Define what ready means

Ask whether the premises are a warm shell, generic fitted office, plug-and-play workspace or previously occupied floor. Record furniture, rooms, cabling, active systems and exclusions.

A space can look finished while power, network, access control, statutory approvals or landlord works remain incomplete.

Check capacity against the team

Complete a measured test-fit and inventory audit. Validate seats, meeting rooms, collaboration, support areas, server or equipment rooms, accessibility, circulation and growth.

Inspect condition and useful life of furniture, lighting, flooring, ceilings, HVAC distribution, fire devices, pantry equipment and washrooms.

Test technology and building systems

Confirm electrical load, backup interface, cooling at actual density, fresh air, telecom carrier readiness, pathways, Wi-Fi design dependencies and equipment-room resilience.

Witness or review testing where operations are critical. Clarify which faults are landlord, seller, operator or tenant responsibility.

Approvals and handover

Request current occupancy, fire and other applicable approvals, approved fit-out drawings, as-built information, asset warranties and maintenance records.

Build a joint snag list with acceptance criteria and dates. Do not set the business move solely from a marketing possession date.

Commercial comparison

Clarify whether the fit-out is included in rent, sold, licensed or maintained by another party. Review deposits, lock-in, escalation, maintenance, parking, reinstatement and end-of-life obligations.

Compare faster occupancy value with customisation constraints, residual asset life and long-term cost. Million Minds Tech City availability must be confirmed unit by unit.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

What does ready-to-move office mean?

The term varies. Confirm exact fit-out, furniture, systems, approvals and the date the premises can support operations.

Can a fitted office be occupied immediately?

Not necessarily; IT, security, testing, approvals, snags and business move planning may remain.

Should we conduct a test-fit?

Yes. Existing furniture and room layouts may not match the team's programme or code requirements.

Is ready-to-move space available at Million Minds Tech City?

Request a current unit-level inventory and handover schedule from the authorised leasing team.

What documents should be requested?

Approved and as-built drawings, system schedules, approvals, warranties, maintenance history, inventory and snag closure.
